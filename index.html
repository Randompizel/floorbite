How it works

Three steps, and the last one runs on its own.

  1. Pick a collection

    Choose the one NFT collection your coin is tied to. The choice is fixed at launch.

  2. Trade the coin

    Every trade produces creator fees. 80% of them go to the coin’s vault.

  3. The vault buys the floor

    The vault can spend only on your collection, so fees become floor purchases.

Where the fees go

Most of the fees are locked into buying NFTs. The rest goes to whoever launched the coin.

80% to the vault20%

The vault is a one-purpose wallet. It cannot send funds anywhere else.

Then choose what happens to the NFTs

The NFTs stay in the vault. The coin’s holders can see the growing stash at any time.

Questions

Can the vault buy anything else?

No. It is bound to the single collection you picked, so it has no way to spend on other tokens or send funds out.

Does this make the floor go up?

Buying listings removes supply from the market, which can lift the floor price. It is not guaranteed, and a floor can also fall.

Can I change the collection later?

No. The collection and the NFT destination are set when the coin is launched.

Who can launch a coin?

Anyone with a collection in mind.
